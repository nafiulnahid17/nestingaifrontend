Vexora AI by Atherious Labs

Vexora AI is a creative production platform from Bangladesh-based Atherious Labs, designed to bring artwork preparation, vector workflows and Adobe Illustrator automation into a single workspace.

Artwork and vector workflow

The web workspace accepts PNG, JPEG and WebP design uploads up to 50 MB, shows a browser preview and provides 1K, 2K and 4K quality presets. The intended production workflow uses an approved artwork as the master source for subsequent vector preparation.

Native Adobe Illustrator connection

The platform is designed to connect to a production engine, automation engine and authorized desktop bridge. Artwork generation, vector processing and export depend on connected services. Availability is shown in the workspace; an unconfigured service cannot complete production jobs.

Who develops Vexora AI?

Vexora AI is developed by Atherious Labs, an AI and product research lab in Bangladesh.
